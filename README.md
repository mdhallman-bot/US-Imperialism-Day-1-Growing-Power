# America's Growing Power — Independent U.S. History Investigation

**Dates:** November 30 (A-day) / December 1 (B-day), 2026  
**Status:** DRAFT — NOT RELEASE-CHECKED; teacher must test saving, restoration, PDFs and external images.

## Student experience
One section at a time. Historical context and vocabulary → prediction → evidence examination → short analytical explanations and stakeholder-power reasoning → revised prediction → reveal. Ranked-motive tasks capture an initial and a revised order plus concise written justifications. A student can navigate back without losing entered work. The Open Door / China extension is optional.

The historical country outlines used in the Oregon and Pacific maps are local GeoJSON data, not hand-drawn coastlines. Orange route segments connect historically grounded key USS Oregon stops and are an **approximate course**, not an exact navigational reconstruction. Other images are historical source reproductions from credited institutions; an external image may be unavailable on a restrictive school network.

## Student data and technology
- Immutable lesson namespace: `us-imperialism-growing-power-2026`
- Backend tab: `Imperialism_Growing_Power`
- The Apps Script Web App URL supplied by the teacher is embedded in `app.js`.
- No names, emails, or school IDs; student alias requires two letters and two digits.
- Local response state; revision-controlled cloud POST followed by readback; conflict protection.
- Local jsPDF library (`assets/jspdf.umd.min.js`) and separate Emergency PDF.
- Completion grade counts required responses, not quality.
- The external public image hosts are not essential to saving/PDF, but must be verified on school devices.
